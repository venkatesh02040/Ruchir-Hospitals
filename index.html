About Ruchir Hospitals

Family-Led Doctors, Trusted by Modasa for Over 35 Years

Founded in 1987 by Dr. Jitendra Patil, a distinguished ENT surgeon, Ruchir Hospitals has grown into a multi-specialty family practice. With Dr. Kanjana Patil (MDS – Dental Surgery) and the next generation Dr. Viranchi Patil (General Medicine) and Dr. Nidhi Patil (Gynecology) we bring expert, ethical care to every patient we serve.

  • Serving Modasa and Aravalli district since 1987
  • Four specialist doctors under one roof
  • Ethical care at affordable costs always
  • ENT, Dental, General Medicine & Gynecology
  • Trusted by over 2 lakh residents across the district
