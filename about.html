Our Story

A Legacy of Care Built Over 35 Years

Ruchir Hospitals was established in 1987 by Dr. Jitendra Patil, a highly respected ENT surgeon who set out to bring ethical, high-quality healthcare to the people of Modasa and Aravalli district. Over the decades, his wife Dr. Kanjana Patil — an MDS Surgical Specialist in Dentistry joined the practice, strengthening the Hospitals's multi-specialty foundation.

Today, the next generation carries the legacy forward. Dr. Viranchi Patil leads General Medicine while Dr. Nidhi Patil heads the Gynecology department together forming a cohesive family of four dedicated doctors, all united by one shared mission: world-class healthcare at affordable costs.

  • Founded in 1987, serving Modasa for over 35 years
  • Four specialist doctors across ENT, Dental, Medicine & Gynecology
  • Trusted by patients across all sections of society
  • Committed to ethical practices and patient transparency
  • Visioning expansion into a full multi-specialty hospital

Meet Our Founder

Dr. Jitendra Patil — The Man Who Started It All

Dr. Jitendra Patil is a distinguished ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat) Surgeon who founded Ruchir Hospitals in Modasa in 1987 with a singular vision: to provide world-class, ethical medical care to every patient regardless of their background or means. What began as a single-specialty ENT practice has, under his leadership and values, grown into the trusted multi-specialty Hospitals it is today.

With decades of surgical experience, Dr. Patil is widely regarded as one of the most respected ENT specialists in Aravalli district. His commitment to honest diagnosis, affordable treatment, and genuine patient welfare has defined Ruchir Hospitals's identity and continues to guide the next generation of Patil doctors who practise alongside him.

"Medicine is not a business — it is a calling. At Ruchir Hospitals, we have always believed that every patient deserves honest counsel, expert care, and the dignity of being truly heard. That principle has never changed in 35 years, and it never will."

— Dr. Jitendra Patil, ENT Surgeon & Founder, Ruchir Hospitals
