Compassionate Women's Healthcare – Expert Gynecology & Obstetric Services

The Gynecology Department at Ruchir Hospitals is led by Dr. Nidhi Patil, a qualified gynecologist who represents the next generation of the Patil family's commitment to holistic and ethical healthcare. Dr. Nidhi provides a safe, supportive, and confidential space for women of all ages from adolescents navigating menstrual health to expectant mothers and women approaching menopause. At Ruchir Hospitals, we understand that women's health requires sensitivity, privacy, and Hospitalsal excellence in equal measure. Our gynecology department is designed to offer exactly that, combining modern diagnostic and treatment capabilities with a deeply compassionate approach that has long defined the Ruchir Hospitals ethos.

Dr. Nidhi Patil combines thorough Hospitalsal expertise with a warm, reassuring manner that puts even the most apprehensive patients at ease. From routine check-ups to complex gynecological conditions and antenatal care, she ensures that every woman receives the highest standard of personalised care throughout every stage of her life.

  • Comprehensive antenatal care, pregnancy monitoring, and postnatal follow-up for expectant mothers.
  • Diagnosis and management of menstrual disorders including irregular periods, heavy bleeding, and dysmenorrhoea.
  • Polycystic Ovarian Disease (PCOD/PCOS) management through lifestyle, hormonal therapy, and personalised care plans.
  • Family planning consultations and contraceptive counselling tailored to individual needs and preferences.
  • Gynaecological screenings including Pap smear, pelvic examination, and ultrasound assessments.
  • Menopause management including hormonal evaluation and guidance for a healthy menopausal transition.

Why regular gynecological care matters for every woman?

Women's health needs evolve at every stage of life, and regular gynecological consultations are essential for staying ahead of potential concerns. Many conditions including cervical abnormalities, ovarian cysts, fibroids, and hormonal imbalances are most effectively managed when detected early. At Ruchir Hospitals, Dr. Nidhi Patil encourages women to prioritise annual gynecological check-ups as part of their overall wellness routine. Whether you are a young woman seeking guidance on menstrual health, a mother planning her family, or a woman navigating menopause, you will find attentive, respectful, and expert care in our Gynecology Department. Our Hospitals has been a trusted healthcare partner for families in Modasa since 1987, and we are proud to extend that legacy into women's specialised healthcare.
