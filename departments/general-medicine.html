Trusted General Medicine – Comprehensive Primary & Preventive Healthcare

The General Medicine Department at Ruchir Hospitals is led by Dr. Viranchi Patil, the next generation of the Patil family's legacy of ethical and compassionate healthcare. As a general physician, Dr. Viranchi brings a fresh, contemporary approach to primary care while upholding the Hospitals's core values of affordability and patient trust. This department serves as the first point of contact for patients across all age groups diagnosing acute illnesses, managing chronic conditions, conducting health screenings, and coordinating care with specialist departments within the Hospitals. Patients visiting for anything from a common fever to complex multi-system diseases receive thorough, evidence-based care under one trusted roof.

Dr. Viranchi Patil approaches each consultation with meticulous attention and a holistic perspective. He evaluates not just the presenting symptoms but also the patient's lifestyle, medical history, and overall health to arrive at the most accurate diagnosis and the most appropriate treatment plan.

  • Diagnosis and treatment of acute illnesses including fever, infections, respiratory conditions, and gastrointestinal disorders.
  • Management of chronic diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, and asthma.
  • Routine health check-ups, preventive screenings, and general wellness consultations for all age groups.
  • Vaccination services and immunisation schedules for children and adults.
  • Coordination and referral management with ENT, Dental, and Gynecology departments within the Hospitals.
  • Lifestyle counselling for weight management, stress reduction, and chronic disease prevention.

Why choose a family general physician for your primary care?

A trusted general physician is the foundation of good healthcare for every family. Dr. Viranchi Patil understands the medical histories of entire families served by Ruchir Hospitals over generations, allowing him to provide highly personalised and continuity-based care. Whether you come in for a seasonal illness or need long-term management of a chronic condition, Dr. Viranchi ensures that every patient receives the same level of focused attention and thorough care. The General Medicine department at Ruchir Hospitals bridges the gap between specialist care and routine healthcare needs, making quality medicine genuinely accessible to the people of Modasa and the Aravalli district.
