Advanced Dental Care – Comprehensive Oral Health & Surgical Expertise

The Dental Department at Ruchir Hospitals is led by Dr. Kanjana Patil, a highly qualified dentist and MDS (Master of Dental Surgery) surgical specialist. With her joining the Hospitals, Ruchir Hospitals expanded into a complete family healthcare destination, offering both general dentistry and advanced oral surgical procedures under one roof. Dr. Kanjana Patil combines Hospitalsal excellence with a patient-centric approach, ensuring that every patient from young children to senior adults receives thorough, pain-free, and affordable dental care. Our modern dental setup is equipped to handle routine check-ups, cosmetic treatments, and complex surgical interventions with precision and care.

Dr. Kanjana Patil's surgical specialisation sets our dental department apart. Beyond routine dental care, she brings expertise in oral and maxillofacial procedures, making Ruchir Hospitals capable of handling complex dental cases that would otherwise require referral to a bigger city centre.

  • General dentistry including check-ups, cleanings, fillings, and tooth extractions for all age groups.
  • Advanced oral surgical procedures including impacted wisdom tooth removal and surgical extractions.
  • Cosmetic dentistry services including tooth whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers.
  • Fixed and removable prosthetics crowns, bridges, dentures, and implant-supported restorations.
  • Root canal treatment (RCT) and endodontic care to save infected or damaged teeth.
  • Paediatric dentistry and preventive care including fluoride treatments and pit-and-fissure sealants.

Why is regular dental care important?

Oral health is closely linked to your overall systemic health. Untreated dental infections, gum disease, and decay can contribute to complications affecting the heart, diabetes management, and respiratory health. At Ruchir Hospitals, Dr. Kanjana Patil encourages patients to attend routine dental examinations every six months so that problems can be caught early, treated conservatively, and prevented from escalating into more complex and costly procedures. Whether you are visiting for a simple cleaning or a full mouth rehabilitation, our dental team ensures you leave with a healthier smile and complete peace of mind.
