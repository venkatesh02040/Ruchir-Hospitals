Expert ENT Care – Ear, Nose & Throat Specialist Services

The ENT Department at Ruchir Hospitals has been the cornerstone of our practice since the Hospitals's founding in 1987. Led by Dr. Jitendra Patil, a highly experienced ENT surgeon with decades of hands-on expertise, our department offers comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for all conditions related to the ear, nose, and throat. From common ailments such as sinusitis and tonsillitis to complex surgical interventions, our team is equipped to deliver world-class care with compassion and precision. We serve patients across Akshar Bhavan, Nagarpalika Office Road, Behind ST Station, Lakshmi Society, Modasa, and the surrounding regions, having built a legacy of trust and ethical medical practice over nearly four decades.

Dr. Jitendra Patil brings unmatched surgical skill and a patient-first approach to every consultation. Whether you are experiencing chronic hearing loss, nasal blockages, voice disorders, or require a surgical procedure, our ENT department provides accurate diagnosis and personalised treatment plans tailored to your needs.

  • Comprehensive diagnosis and management of ear disorders including hearing loss, ear infections, and tinnitus.
  • Expert treatment for nasal conditions such as sinusitis, nasal polyps, deviated septum, and allergic rhinitis.
  • Throat and voice care including tonsillitis, laryngitis, voice disorders, and swallowing difficulties.
  • Surgical expertise in endoscopic sinus surgery, tonsillectomy, adenoidectomy, and ear surgeries.
  • Paediatric ENT services for children with recurrent ear infections, adenoid problems, and speech-related concerns.
  • Preventive consultations and regular follow-up care to maintain long-term ENT health.

When should you visit an ENT specialist?

Many patients delay seeking ENT care, mistaking chronic symptoms for minor inconveniences. If you experience persistent ear pain, frequent sinus headaches, difficulty swallowing, prolonged hoarseness, or recurring throat infections, it is important to consult a specialist promptly. At Ruchir Hospitals, Dr. Jitendra Patil provides thorough evaluations to identify the root cause of your symptoms and recommends the most effective course of treatment surgical or non-surgical to restore your quality of life. With nearly four decades of experience, patients from across the Aravalli district trust our ENT department for dependable, affordable, and ethical healthcare.
