Dr. Nidhi Patil

Gynecologist, Ruchir Hospitals

A compassionate and skilled gynecologist dedicated to providing every woman with the dignity, privacy, and expert care she deserves, across every stage of her life.

Women's health is deeply personal, and every woman who walks into my consultation deserves to feel heard, respected, and safe. At Ruchir Hospitals, I work to create that environment one where women feel confident enough to speak openly about their concerns, knowing they will receive honest, expert, and completely confidential care. Whether it is a young girl navigating her first visit, an expectant mother, or a woman managing a complex gynaecological condition, I am committed to being a steady, trusted presence in their healthcare journey. This is more than a profession for me it is a responsibility I take with great pride.

Skills & Expertise

Dr. Nidhi Patil brings clinical excellence and deep empathy to gynaecological care. Her expertise spans obstetrics, reproductive health, hormonal management, and complex gynaecological conditions all delivered with the discretion and sensitivity that women's healthcare demands.

Medical Treatments by Dr. Nidhi Patil

Dr. Nidhi Patil provides end-to-end gynaecological care tailored to each woman's unique needs and life stage. From routine screening and family planning to antenatal management and complex hormonal conditions, every patient receives a personalised, evidence-based treatment plan in a safe and confidential setting.

  • Qualified gynecologist serving as part of the next generation of the Patil family's legacy at Ruchir Hospitals, founded in 1987.
  • Comprehensive antenatal and postnatal care including pregnancy monitoring, nutritional guidance, and birth planning support.
  • Expert management of menstrual disorders, PCOD/PCOS, uterine fibroids, ovarian cysts, and other gynaecological conditions.
  • Family planning counselling, contraceptive guidance, and gynaecological screenings including Pap smear and pelvic ultrasound.
  • Known for a warm, empathetic approach that ensures every woman feels comfortable, respected, and fully supported throughout her care.
