Dr. Kanjana Patil

Dentist & MDS Surgical Specialist, Ruchir Hospitals

A highly qualified dentist and MDS surgical specialist who brings advanced oral surgical expertise to Ruchir Hospitals, ensuring patients receive world-class dental care close to home.

A healthy smile is more than aesthetics it is a window to your overall well-being. I joined Ruchir Hospitals with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between general dental care and advanced oral surgery for the people of Modasa and the wider Aravalli district. Many patients had to travel far for complex procedures. Today, they do not have to. From a child's first dental visit to intricate surgical interventions, our dental department is built on the same values that have defined this hospital since 1987 ethics, affordability, and genuine compassion for every patient who walks through our doors.

Skills & Expertise

Dr. Kanjana Patil's MDS surgical specialisation places her expertise well beyond routine dentistry. She combines precise surgical technique with a gentle, patient-centred approach, handling everything from complex tooth extractions to full mouth rehabilitation with equal care and thoroughness.

Medical Treatments by Dr. Kanjana Patil

Dr. Kanjana Patil offers a comprehensive range of dental and oral surgical treatments. Her approach prioritises preserving natural teeth wherever possible, and every treatment plan is carefully discussed with the patient so they understand their options and feel fully confident before proceeding.

  • Qualified dentist holding an MDS (Master of Dental Surgery) degree with specialisation in oral surgical procedures.
  • Expertise in general dentistry, oral surgery, root canal treatment, prosthetics, cosmetic dentistry, and paediatric dental care.
  • Brings advanced oral surgical capability to Modasa, reducing the need for patients to travel to larger cities for complex procedures.
  • Known for a gentle, reassuring chairside manner that puts anxious patients especially children completely at ease.
  • Committed to preventive dental care and patient education, helping families maintain healthy smiles across all age groups.
